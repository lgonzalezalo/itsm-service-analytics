ITSM Service Analytics

Synthetic data (5,000 tickets, Jan–Jun 2026). SLA targets are my own assumptions, not a client's.

1. Service health

Rates are weighted by ticket count. P90 cannot be combined across groups, so it is shown per team and priority.

2. Load per team

Tickets opened per month, and effort booked per team. Effort is a field I invented in the generator (minutes actually worked), not elapsed time.

3. Automation candidates

Ranked by total effort booked (resolved tickets only, uncategorised excluded). This ranking reflects the generator's parameters, and it does not score how automatable each case is.
